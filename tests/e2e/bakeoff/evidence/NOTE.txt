R1-WP3 evidence notes
=====================

Large PNG/JPEG blobs are gitignored under this folder. A small
`*-small.png` may be committed if a capture stays under ~100 KiB.

If the editor GUI cannot launch in this environment, the driver records
SKIP for visual evidence and may write a path under %TEMP% instead of
committing a huge screenshot.

Human editor (no MCP): repo-root `hh-godot-editor.bat` opens the
`godot/test-projects/minimal-2d` fixture on Godot 4.7.1-stable.
Do not enable MCP there. Bake-off copies live under
`tests/e2e/bakeoff/work/` (gitignored).
