GAS CAN (https://superfighters.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_Can)
- Small hazard, metal, flammable.
- On destroyed (shot, burned, blown up): releases FIRE.
- Players next to can set to level 2 fire.
- Area denial / burn flammables nearby.
- Mines/C4 nearby may snuff fire (Deluxe note).

EXPLOSIVE BARREL (https://superfighters.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_Barrel)
- ~10 HP but can detonate on single Uzi hit (Deluxe quirk).
- Explodes when shot or fighter falls on it from distance.
- Destroys barrel, spreads fire nearby.
- Wreck launches; second explosion when wreck hit again.
- Poor cover: direct blast hurts more than absorbed bullet.

WOODEN objects (Category:Wooden): flammable, spread fire via debris.
