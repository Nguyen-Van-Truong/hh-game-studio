Source: https://superfighters.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Movements (text snapshot 2026-10-16)

Roll: Down + direction. While rolling, chance to dodge bullets. On fire: one or two rolls put out fire.

Dive: Crouch while sprinting. Dodge bullets; after dive performs roll (can put out fire). No fall damage.
Grab enemies by diving into them (shield / pit throw). Interrupted by timed melee or some shotguns/flare gun.

Block: Blocks attacks and thrown items; timed melee block can absorb/deflect bullets (metal weapons).

Grab: Punch ~17 HP or throw; short delay; range includes slightly above player.

Cover: Touch chest-high object + direction again; your bullets pass, enemy bullets blocked.

Disarm: Hit opponent while aiming gun → weapon knocked opposite facing.

Other: ladder drop/jump, ledge grab, grenade jump, baseball bat shooting ball.
