# SA-05 — provisional bullet-time power

This is implementation evidence only. It does not claim that the current
public Y8 build has been measured or that Superagent has reached parity.
SA-02 remains the current gate and no Classic checkbox is ticked.

## Implemented slice

- The original relay-yard data now has clean-room `bullet_time_5` and
  `bullet_time_10` pickups with 300- and 600-fixed-tick durations. Both use the
  same Down+Punch interaction path as weapons and grenades.
- A fighter carries one power charge. Pressing the power key consumes it and
  starts bullet time; pressing the key again cancels the effect early.
- The activator keeps the normal 60-Hz step while the other fighter advances
  every second fixed tick. The active owner, divisor and remaining duration are
  exposed in the immutable match snapshot and canonical replay hash.
- A melee hit against an aiming fighter clears that fighter's gun slot and
  ammunition and records a short-lived disarm event. The observed research only
  documents the endpoint (the gun disappears), so no recoverable dropped weapon
  is claimed here.
- HUD output shows the power count and active Bullet Time state. Future hero
  abilities remain unbound and locked until SA-14.

## Open gaps

The duration and half-step divisor are provisional engineering values. The
bullet trajectory timing, complete weapon catalog, slot/drop semantics, rocket
riding, spawn law, and owner side-by-side review remain open. The tip
research is a documented lead; it is not a replacement for SA-02 observations.
