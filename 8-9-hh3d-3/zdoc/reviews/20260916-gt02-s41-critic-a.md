## Findings

### P1 — TX15 safe-write availability remains an explicit acceptance blocker

`8-9-hh3d-3/studio/host/core/safe_open.py:154-157` reports
`safe_write=false` and `atomic_replace=false`. The instance capability at
`safe_open.py:181-184` reports the same, and every public mutation method at
`safe_open.py:236-246` raises `UNSUPPORTED_SAFE_OPEN_WINDOWS` without opening
or writing a native handle.

This matches the authoritative plan’s path-protection contract: the Windows
primitive must protect the final handle and identity across validate/open/
replace, and if that primitive is unavailable the result is a GAP rather than
an accepted capability (`8-9-hh3d-3/zdoc/8-9-godot-blender-agent-studio-plan.txt`,
§2.2 and TX15). The plan’s GT-02 DoD also states that the fail-closed
`UNSUPPORTED` response does not remove the availability gap.

The adversarial Windows test is evidence for retaining the gate, not for
passing it. `test_safe_open.py:271-291` creates a second hardlink while an
exclusive handle is held and deliberately performs no `WriteFile`; it then
asserts that mutation remains unsupported. The swap and junction tests likewise
show rejection/no outside effect, but they do not provide an identity-preserving
writer or atomic replacement path.

### P1 — Private staging and native AppContainer evidence do not close the public writer gap

`private_store.py:1-13` explicitly describes the store as experimental,
broker-trusted staging and says that public `safe_write`/`atomic_replace`
remain false. It also states that IPC/sandbox launcher, activation, namespace
durability acknowledgement, and automatic recovery are absent. The S39 audit
therefore correctly limits the native run to a fixed fixture endpoint and
does not claim a production project-root writer or a Godot/Blender consumer.

The native `7/7` result proves the tested AppContainer boundary and selector
protocol behavior. It does not establish TX15’s required positive mutation
property for caller-selected project paths, nor does it establish atomic
replacement under a same-account hardlink/reparse/alias race. Treating that
boundary result as safe-write availability would contradict the source
capability flags and the S40 counterexample record.

### P1 — Acceptance evidence is incomplete even though the bounded candidate tests are green

The plan requires two independent frozen-source critic verdicts for GT-02.
The S39 audit records zero independent signatures, and the candidate metadata
marks itself not reviewed. The closure therefore cannot be promoted from
CANDIDATE to ACCEPTED on the current evidence. The missing signatures are
independent of the test counts and remain required after any source change.

## Verdict

`TICK=no`

S39-02 is not acceptance-ready. Keep GT-02 at `CANDIDATE`, keep the Windows

requires a fresh closure with a proved Windows-safe mutation primitive,
source-bound evidence for that primitive, and two independent critic verdicts
on that exact closure.
