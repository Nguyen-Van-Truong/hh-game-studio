  satisfied here. TX15's safe-open availability GAP remains real and must be

  native consumer boundary before dispatch. The frozen report already states
  this limitation and the live fixture host currently owns wire admission.

  symbolic-link fixtures, and the Linux-only negative capability test was not
  applicable. Junction/hardlink attacks did execute on Windows. No Linux
  safe-open, portability, WAN, human, render or release acceptance is claimed.

  not establish durable Godot/Blender world-state recovery across engine death,
  arbitrary generated-script sandbox execution or a distributed writer service.
  Unsupported operations remain denied until their owning adapter gate.
- Session/output checks exercise the registered credentials and encoded variants
  in the tests; they are not a proof about every possible transformation of
  arbitrary data. Trusted host journal storage is not a public file-write API.
