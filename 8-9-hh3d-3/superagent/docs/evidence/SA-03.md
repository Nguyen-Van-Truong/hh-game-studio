# SA-03 evidence

Status: **PENDING_VERIFY**.

This package adds a static deterministic locomotion core for two fighters. The
simulation advances integer-quantized state at 60 Hz, reads immutable
`InputFrame` records, models placeholder platforms, ladders and a pit, and
includes fighter/world timers plus deterministic RNG counters in its snapshot
and hash. `tests/trace_core.gd` is source-only and has not been executed while
the shared GT-06 measurement lock is present.

The tuning values and seed are explicitly **UNVERIFIED_PLACEHOLDER** values.
They do not claim Y8 observation, parity, or production balance. Godot import,
headless trace execution, process-exit capture, and critic review remain
pending after the lock is cleared.
