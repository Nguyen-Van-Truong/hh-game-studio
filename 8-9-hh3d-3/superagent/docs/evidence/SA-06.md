# SA-06 — provisional world props and burning slice

This is implementation evidence only. It does not claim measured Y8 parity and
does not close the SA-06 gate. SA-02 remains the current plan gate.

## Implemented slice

- Relay Yard authors four clean-room props with explicit material, health and
  explosive fields: two wood crates, one steel barrel and one glass panel.
- `MatchSession` owns mutable copies. Read-only snapshots expose active,
  health, max health, material and explosive state; replay hashes include the
  prop state and the fire/roll state.
- Live melee and held/release shots damage the nearest authored prop. Destroying
  the barrel emits a bounded explosion, damages nearby fighters and can ignite
  a living fighter. Static cover remains unchanged after a prop is destroyed.
- A grounded Down plus horizontal press enters a real short-collider roll using
  `CharacterBody2D`; gravity and floor readback remain active. A roll is blocked
  while airborne or knocked down, and fire clears only after the fighter really
  enters the grounded roll. Burning applies periodic damage and expires without
  an extra post-expiry tick.
- An airborne Down plus horizontal press enters a bounded dive tackle. The same
  live prop path now lets the dive break the authored glass panel, while fighter
  hit resolution records dive damage/knockdown and the move's bounded
  invulnerability. This is a clean-room implementation slice, not a measured
  Y8 claim.
- The presentation draws active prop rows and burning state from the snapshot;
  it does not mutate simulation state.
